# Assembly audit, 2026-09-03

Build: nanometa_live 0.17.2, nanometanf v1.9.0. Plan:
`~/.claude/plans/make-a-plan-to-cheeky-crayon.md`. Kit: `~/nanometa-audit-r5/`.

## Why

Assembly became operator-reachable on 2026-08-17 (`f5e0023`). It is the only
feature in the product that can run, succeed, report no failures, and publish a
number that is not a result. The round-5 drills saw Flye emit 8-27 kb of contigs
from real-time batches and the Reports tab render them with an N50; round 5
suppressed that by disabling assembly in real time (C8) without answering
whether the feature works at all.

## Method

Stage 0 of the plan: measure before changing anything. Runs use the round-5 kit
(`run.sh`, `feed.sh`, `set_form.js`, `snapshot_launch.sh`) against the Bioshield
database and the demo corpus, driven through the real GUI form so the launch
path is the operator's. Depth is measured by
`scripts/audit_assembly_depth.py`, new here.

## A5 — the depth census

`scripts/audit_assembly_depth.py` sums the bases Kraken2 assigned to each taxid
and divides by the length of the reference genome already in the cache. It
reports two figures per target: the **exact** node, which is what
`EXTRACT_READS_BY_TAXID` selects (`$1 == "C" && $3 == taxid`), and the **clade**,
which is what an assembly of that organism could actually use.

Run over the full-corpus batch results:

```
sample           taxid  exact rd  clade rd  clade Mb  genome Mb   exact   clade  verdict
barcode06      4007169       279      1051     3.647       1.87   0.23x   1.95x  decline   Francisella tularensis
barcode07      4007169       278       992     3.337       1.87   0.22x   1.78x  decline   Francisella tularensis
barcode05      4007169       230       763     2.432       1.87   0.18x   1.30x  decline   Francisella tularensis
barcode08      4007169       197       661     2.055       1.87   0.14x   1.10x  decline   Francisella tularensis
barcode07      4000800       223       223     0.648       4.95   0.13x   0.13x  decline   Salmonella enterica
...
barcode08      4005020         8         8     0.016       5.50   0.00x   0.00x  decline   Bacillus_A anthracis
```

**0 of 25 (sample, taxid) pairs reach 30x**, the charitable floor for a usable
ONT draft. The best target in the corpus, *F. tularensis* in barcode06, reaches
**1.95x** — a fifteen-fold shortfall. *B. anthracis*, a select agent the verdict
banner calls ACTION REQUIRED on, sits at 0.00x with 8 reads.

Whole-sample totals are 9.5-15.6 Mb per barcode, 6.5-11.0 Mb of it classified,
spread across a community.

**This is the load-bearing measurement of the audit.** On this data the correct
behaviour is to decline and explain, which the feature cannot currently express.
A depth gate is therefore not a safety margin bolted onto a working feature; it
is the feature's primary output for the data this tool actually sees.

### A5b — exact-taxid extraction captures a fraction of the organism

`EXTRACT_READS_BY_TAXID` matches the taxid exactly. Kraken2 assigns reads to the
most specific node it can, so for an organism with subspecies the reads scatter:

```
12.83  763  230  S   4007169    Francisella tularensis
 8.91  530  530  S1  4007187      Francisella tularensis holarctica
 0.02    1    1  S1  4007186      Francisella tularensis tularensis
```

Exact matching takes 230 of 763 reads in barcode05, and **279 of 1051 (27%) in
barcode06**. The other 72% sit on the subspecies nodes and describe the same
genome.

This is not an assembly finding. `EXTRACT_READS_BY_TAXID` is the read selector
for **confirmatory validation**, so today's BLAST and minimap2 confirmation of
*F. tularensis* is aligning about a quarter of the organism's reads. Targeted
assembly must select the clade, and validation should be re-examined on the same
grounds. It matches the species-includes-subspecies rule already established in
`core/taxonomy/ranks.py`, which the pipeline-side selector does not follow.

## A1 — a real Flye run on the full corpus

Batch mode, by_barcode, the whole demo corpus, `assembler: flye`, started through
the GUI. Run recorded `final_status: completed`, `processes_failed: 0`, no failed
tasks. Five assemblies published in about six minutes.

| sample | contigs | total bp | N50 | largest | Flye's mean coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| barcode05 | 29 | 227,821 | 10,041 | 19,034 | 7 |
| barcode06 | 63 | 570,013 | 12,368 | 38,949 | 5 |
| barcode07 | 64 | 510,073 | 11,143 | 26,199 | 6 |
| barcode08 | 31 | 238,358 | 35,194 | 35,194 | 6 |
| unclassified | 5 | 35,552 | 6,866 | 15,806 | - |

**Flye did nothing wrong.** It assembled what it was given and stated the
coverage plainly in its own log. The largest result, 0.57 Mb across 63 contigs,
is a fraction of any single genome in a sample containing several. The number a
reader needs in order to know that — coverage 5 — is the one the product drops.

### The dropped number

`bin/assembly_to_canonical.py` records per-contig coverage but the summary block
does not carry it:

```
summary keys: total_contigs, total_length, largest_contig, n50, l50,
              circular_contigs, gc_content
per-contig:   name, length, coverage, is_circular, gc_content
barcode06 per-contig coverage: n=63, median 4.0, max 32.0
```

`build_assembly_panel` renders exactly the summary fields as KPI tiles, so the
operator sees "63 contigs, N50 12,368" and never sees "median coverage 4". The
per-contig table shows a Depth column, but only for the top ten contigs and
without a summary figure to judge them against.

**This is the cheapest high-value fix in the audit.** A mean or median coverage
in the summary and a coverage KPI tile require no new pipeline machinery: the
numbers already exist in the file the writer parses.

## A4 — the failure-invisibility chain

Established by reading; each link independently verified.

| Link | Location | State |
|---|---|---|
| Ignored exit | `conf/error_isolation.config` | FLYE `errorStrategy 'ignore'` on exit 1/2, so a failed assembly leaves the run successful |
| No marker | `bin/nanometanf_lost_input_marker.sh` | wired to CHOPPER, FASTP_STREAMING and the KRAKEN2 processes; FLYE and MINIASM have no `afterScript`, so nothing lands in `pipeline_info/lost_inputs/` |
| Not a failed sample | `workflows/nanometanf.nf:521-562` | assembly is not a term in the `failed_samples` computation |
| Manifest asserts files | `bin/write_manifest.py:140-142` | `sorted(f"{s}.assembly_stats.json" for s in samples) if args.assembler else []` — filenames are **derived from the sample list**, not discovered, so with assembly on and every task failed the manifest lists files that do not exist |
| GUI silent | `app/tabs/reports_helpers.py:149-197` | `build_assembly_panel([])` returns `""`; its docstring reads `[] => assembly not run, render nothing`, so off, running and failed render identically |

Two further findings from the same reading:

- **miniasm cannot reach the Reports tab at all.** `CANONICAL_ASSEMBLY_WRITER`
  is gated `assembler == 'flye'`, so selecting miniasm produces no canonical
  JSON, while the form says "results appear on the Reports tab". It is also
  absent from error isolation, so a single exit-1 ends the whole run — which is
  what the two round-5 miniasm batch runs did (`final_status: error`, zero
  assembly files).
- **Three documented knobs are unreachable from the GUI.** The A1 launch omits
  `genome_size`, `flye_meta` and `sequencing_mode` entirely. `genome_size` is
  dead on the pipeline side too: read into a local in the assembly subworkflow
  and never passed to Flye.

## What this changes in the plan

The staging holds, with two amendments:

1. **A coverage KPI moves into Stage 1.** It is a reporting fix, not a gating
   feature, and it is what makes the current output honest at the lowest cost.
2. **Clade-aware read selection is added to Stage 2** and flagged for validation
   as well, since `EXTRACT_READS_BY_TAXID` is shared. Targeted assembly that
   selects the exact node would start from a quarter of the available reads.

The `assembly_min_depth` default of 30 is confirmed by A5: nothing in this
corpus approaches it, so the Stage 2 release must lead with this census or the
gate will read as a regression.

## A2 — a real miniasm run, and the P2 fix confirmed

Same corpus and launch path, `assembler: miniasm`. This is also the check round 5
left open: P2 was fixed in nanometanf v1.9.0 and verified in stub mode only,
because MINIASM's version probe cannot run without the tool.

**The P2 fix works on real data.** `MINIMAP2_AVA` produced one all-vs-all PAF per
sample with no input-collision error:

```
barcode05.ava.paf.gz  0.84 MB      barcode07.ava.paf.gz  1.58 MB
barcode06.ava.paf.gz  1.16 MB      barcode08.ava.paf.gz  0.58 MB
```

Three findings follow.

**The intermediate PAF is published into the results tree.** Those 4.1 MB sit in
`<outdir>/minimap2/`, because `conf/modules.config` has no `withName:
'MINIMAP2_AVA'` block and the default publishDir rule resolves its path to
`minimap2`. It is an intermediate that should never leave the work directory, and
it shares a directory name with the validation minimap2 outputs.

**miniasm cannot run on Apple Silicon at all.** The run died with:

```
Failed to create Conda environment
  PackagesNotFoundError: The following packages are not available from current channels:
    - bioconda::miniasm==0.3_r179
  Platform: osx-arm64
```

The GUI offered miniasm as a first-class choice on a machine where it can never
be installed. The 0.15.0 changelog already recorded this for offline bundles; it
applies to every conda run on this hardware.

**The failure discarded a completed screening run.** Classification had finished
for all five samples before assembly ran:

| sample | reads classified |
|---|---|
| barcode05 | 2,696 |
| barcode06 | 3,477 |
| barcode07 | 3,699 |

The run is nonetheless recorded `final_status: error` with `failed_tasks:
['MINIASM (1)']`, because MINIASM is absent from `conf/error_isolation.config`
and so falls back to `finish`. An operator screening for select agents loses the
run's standing because an optional, experimental assembly step could not install
a tool. FLYE is protected by `errorStrategy 'ignore'`; MINIASM and MINIMAP2_AVA
are not, and the asymmetry is undocumented.

This is the most consequential finding of the audit. It is also the cheapest to
fix: three lines in `conf/error_isolation.config`.

## A3 — the real-time per-file explosion, from existing artifacts

The GUI can no longer produce this run, since 0.17.0 drops `enable_assembly` in
real time. The round-5 `rt1` run predates that fix and had assembly on, so its
artifacts answer the question directly. Comparing the same barcodes against A1,
which assembled the whole sample from the same corpus:

| sample | real time (rt1) | batch, full corpus (A1) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| barcode05 | 3 contigs / 7,981 bp | 29 contigs / 227,821 bp | 29x |
| barcode06 | 3 contigs / 16,819 bp | 63 contigs / 570,013 bp | 34x |
| barcode08 | 3 contigs / 26,970 bp | 31 contigs / 238,358 bp | 9x |

28 files were fed across five samples, and **four assembly artifacts survived,
one per sample**. Each holds a single batch's assembly at single-batch scale, not
the sample's. Every earlier batch's assembly was overwritten at the same publish
path, because `ext.prefix` is `meta.id` and `conf/modules.config` publishes all
of them to `${params.outdir}/assembly` with no per-batch subdirectory.

So the real-time path did the work N times, threw away all but the last, and
published a result 9 to 34 times smaller than the sample could give — while the
run reported healthy.

## Stage 1 — done, 2026-09-04

No new capability. Everything below removes a false claim.

**Pipeline** (nanometanf `1d4a536`)

- All three assembly processes carry the same `errorStrategy` and write the
  lost-input marker. FLYE alone was isolated, so the A2 run's miniasm exit
  ended a pipeline whose five samples had already been classified. Assembly
  was also the only isolated process without a marker, which is why an
  ignored failure reached no surface; `read_lost_inputs` already renders
  them, so the marker was the whole GUI wiring.
- The manifest reports the assembly files that exist, taken from the canonical
  writer's own output channel. An empty set with an assembler recorded means
  assembly ran and produced nothing.
- The miniasm join drops a pair rather than manufacturing `[meta, reads, null]`.
- The all-vs-all PAF stays in the work directory.
- Assembly is capped at `maxForks 1`.
- `modules/local/minimap2_ava/` has a test, and it is in the CI list.

**GUI** (nanometa_live `96f9854`)

- A median-depth KPI, and below 10x the panel says the contigs are fragments
  rather than a genome. Verified in a browser on the A1 assembly: *Median depth
  4x* beside the N50 of 10,041.
- `build_assembly_panel` never returns `""`; off, failed, awaiting and produced
  are four distinguishable cards, the failed one derived from the new markers.
- Miniasm withdrawn from the select; the form loader shows flye for a saved
  value and logs why.
- The real-time drop is a launch warning, so the Start toast says so.
- `canonical_loaders.load_canonical_assembly` removed.

**Deferred to Stage 2, deliberately.** The plan put "assembly as a term in
`failed_samples`" in Stage 1, with the predicate "the decision said attempt and
no stats appeared". The decision record does not exist until Stage 2, and the
only Stage-1 predicate available — enabled, reads present, no stats — cannot
tell a failure from a sample with nothing to assemble, which on this corpus is
every sample. Adding it now would manufacture false failures. The lost-input
markers give the visibility in the meantime.
